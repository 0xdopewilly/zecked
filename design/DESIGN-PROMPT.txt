Design a mobile app called ZECKED. Tagline: "Hide it. Crack it. Get Zecked."
"Zeck" is our verb: to crack a stash and win the ZEC ("I just zecked a $20 stash", "your stash got ZECKED 🔓", "Zeck it before kickoff").

WHAT IT IS
ZECKED is a free-to-play social game built on Zcash (the private cryptocurrency, $ZEC). People hide small amounts of ZEC (a "stash") behind a challenge and share it on X and Telegram. The first person to beat the challenge wins the ZEC, and it lands in their private wallet. There are two stash types:
1. RIDDLE STASH: the hider writes a riddle. The first person to type the right answer cracks the stash.
2. PREDICTION STASH: the hider picks a real football match (e.g. Barcelona vs Chelsea). Players predict the exact score or the winner. Predictions lock at kickoff, the result comes in automatically from a live sports feed, and at the final whistle the stash goes to the FIRST person who called it right.
Players never pay to play. The hider puts up the prize. Winners stay anonymous, because Zcash payments are private.

WHO IT'S FOR
Crypto-curious people on their phones, arriving from a link on X or Telegram, plus existing Zcash fans and big holders ("whales") who post big stashes for hype. Many players have never owned crypto, so winning is how they get their first ZEC.

VIBE
Gamey, colorful and joyful, like Duolingo's gamification, Clash Royale's chests and tiers, and a modern sports app's live match cards, mixed with a slick crypto feel. Big rounded cards, chunky type, glossy 3D-ish badges, stickers, confetti. It must feel like a GAME, not a bank or a casino. No casino imagery (no chips, slot machines or dice), and no betting words ("bet", "odds", "wager"). Use "crack", "call it", "predict", "hide", "stash", "vault".
